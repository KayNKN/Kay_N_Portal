drop your game builds in this folder (zip them first).

then point the download button in games.html at the file, e.g.:
  <a class="btn btn--dl" href="downloads/your-game-v1.0.zip" download>▼ download build</a>

tip: keep old versions around (your-game-v1.0.zip, v1.1.zip ...) so players
can roll back if a patch breaks something.
